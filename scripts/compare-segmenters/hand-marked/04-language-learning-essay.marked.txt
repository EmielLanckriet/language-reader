# THE RULE USED, stated so that the marking can be argued with rather than only trusted. A span is
# one word if the reader would want to tap it as a single thing to study, which is this project's
# definition of word-hood (ADR-0002) and not any published standard's. Concretely:
#
#   - a numeral or demonstrative plus its measure word stays together (一个, 半个, 这件, 一句, 一串),
#     because that is the unit a learner meets and looks up
#   - the noun after such a phrase is separate (一句 话, 这件 事, 半个 小时)
#   - a verb plus a resultative complement is separated when both parts are ordinary vocabulary
#     (预料 到, 想 明白, 看 出来 → kept as 看出来 where it is idiomatic), because the learner needs the verb
#   - a fixed multi-syllable pattern stays whole (越来越, 来不及, 一开始, 付不起, 有人)
#   - 不 and 没有 are separate words, not prefixes (不 知道, 没有 变化)
#

# corpus this small, eleven judgment calls can move the score by several points.
